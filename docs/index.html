    Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

    Tap or click “Subscribe in your calendar app” at the top and confirm. If nothing happens, add it by hand:

    1. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, then Calendar, then Calendar Accounts. On newer iOS the path is Settings, Apps, Calendar, Calendar Accounts.
    2. Choose Add Account, then Other, then Add Subscribed Calendar.
    3. Paste the calendar address you copied and save.

    On a Mac, open Calendar and choose File, then New Calendar Subscription, and paste the address.

    Google Calendar
    1. Open Google Calendar in a web browser on a computer.
    2. Next to “Other calendars”, choose Add (+), then From URL.
    3. Paste the calendar address you copied and choose Add calendar.

    The calendar then appears in the Google Calendar app too. Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, often only once or twice a day.

    Outlook
    1. Open Outlook on the web or the new Outlook app and go to Calendar.
    2. Choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
    3. Paste the calendar address you copied, give it a name, and import.
    Thunderbird
    1. In the Calendar tab, choose New Calendar, then On the Network.
    2. Pick iCalendar (ICS) and paste the calendar address.

    What’s in the calendar

    Official League Cups, League Challenges and Prereleases that shops register in the Play! Pokémon Event Locator, within 100 km of Basel by road. That covers Basel, Mulhouse, Zürich, Bern and Luzern.

    League nights, the regular casual play at shops, are their own calendar. They cover shops in Basel and Birsfelden only, and are refreshed from the Event Locator by hand, so a newly announced one can take a few days to show up. Casual meetups that are not in the locator are not included. If a shop or event is missing, open an issue on GitHub.

    Each event also lists driving directions as one-tap links for Google Maps, Apple Maps and Waze, plus a reminder to bring ID and EUR for events in France or Germany. Entry fees, registration and Full or Waiting list flags appear when we know them. The official locator does not publish player numbers, so a missing flag does not mean there is space.
